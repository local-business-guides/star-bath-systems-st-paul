Start with what you want to change

A bathroom remodeler in St Paul can help you think through a room update, from changing the shower to improving how the space works for your household. What to prepare before a remodel

The work depends on your goals and the bathroom itself. Star Bath Systems can discuss bathroom remodeling, walk-in showers, plumbing, and HVAC services, depending on what you need. Before that discussion, gather a few basics:

  • Write down the changes you want, in order of importance.
  • Note any plumbing or heating and cooling concerns you have noticed.
  • Think about how you use the shower and what would make it easier to use.
  • Make a list of questions about the scope and how the work will be handled.

You do not need a finished design to get started. Clear priorities and a straightforward description of the room are useful.
